Buyer checklist questions for rigid gift box

How can the product envelope be documented?

Document the custom rigid gift box envelope with a dimensioned sketch, packed weight, front-facing direction, and photographs that show irregular features. For sets, measure the proposed arrangement so each component and gap is represented in the structural brief.

Why should the final stock be tested in structure?

Testing the final stock in custom rigid gift box reveals whether stiffness, folding, glue response, printing, and opening behavior work together. A flat material sample cannot show panel bowing, tight closures, or finish stress around converted features.

How can teams approve internal fit?

Approve the internal fit of custom rigid gift box by checking contact areas, cavity size, finger clearance, movement, loading direction, removal, and support after normal handling. Record the insert version and product orientation with the prototype.

Prepare a decision-ready project brief

A quotation brief for custom rigid gift box should identify what goes inside, how many components are packed, their measured dimensions and weight, the expected quantity range, chosen sales channel, artwork status, material preference, and any required insert or finish. Note which decisions need supplier input so assumptions are visible.
