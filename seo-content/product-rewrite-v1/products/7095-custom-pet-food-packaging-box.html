Specification questions for custom pet food packaging box

How should the packed item be measured?

Measure the item that will enter custom pet food packaging box at its widest, tallest, and longest points, then record weight and loading orientation. Add caps, cables, accessories, protective wraps, or grouped components so the dieline is based on the real packed envelope.

What makes one board option more suitable than another?

A board option for custom pet food packaging box should be compared by grade, thickness, fold behavior, edge quality, print character, and support of the packed weight. Test those points in the converted structure instead of judging loose swatches alone.

Which checks belong in the prototype assessment?

Assess custom pet food packaging box with the real item and intended pack-out method. Check erection, loading, closure, movement, reveal, removal, reclosure, print position, and any finish at a fold or contact point; record corrections before sign-off.
