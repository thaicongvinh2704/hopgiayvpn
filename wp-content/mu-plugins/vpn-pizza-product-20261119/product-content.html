Specify the Box Around the Pizza You Serve

  • Internal fit. Share the maximum diameter of the baked pizza, the height of its tallest topping and any liner you use. Use the actual baked size even if your menu lists a standard size such as 14 inches. Confirm the usable inside dimensions on the dieline and check lid clearance with a filled sample.
  • Board and closure. E-flute and B-flute corrugated options can be reviewed against the box size, stacking needs and delivery route. Ask staff to fold the sample and check how the front tab seats and how the closed box fits your delivery bag.
  • Print and food contact. Supply your logo artwork and indicate which panels you want printed. Tell us whether hot pizza will touch the board directly and where the boxes will be used. Confirm the proposed board, inks and any liner against those requirements before approval. If your buyer requires a material declaration or test report, tell us which document you need so we can confirm what is available for that box specification.

What to Check on Your Pizza Box Sample

Pack your largest pizza as you normally would, close the lid and stack the filled boxes at the height you use in service. At the end of a typical delivery route, check whether the lid touched the toppings, the tabs stayed closed and the bottom held up. If steam is a concern, compare vented and unvented samples on the same route before selecting the design.

Keep the approved sample and artwork version for each size. Those references are useful when you reorder boxes for the same menu.

Bulk Orders, Lead Times and Packing

For a chain rollout or a distributor order, list quantities by size and artwork, even when the boxes share a logo. If this is a repeat order, include the approved drawing or artwork revision. The quote confirms the minimum and unit price for each version, along with any sampling charges.

For planning, allow 7–15 days for custom sampling and 7–45 days for production after sample approval. Both ranges depend on the order. Shipping time is quoted separately. Carton packing or shrink-wrapped bundles can be agreed for your order. For international enquiries, send the delivery city, postal code, country and required receiving date so we can confirm packing and freight. See the factory's sampling and order terms.

Questions About Bulk Orders

What is the minimum order for custom printed pizza boxes?

The general MOQ for VPN's pizza box range is 1,000 boxes. Your quotation confirms the minimum and whether the order can be split between sizes or artwork versions.

Can we approve a sample before production?

Yes. Confirm whether you need a plain sample to check size and folding, or a printed sample to review the artwork as well. The quotation sets out the sample scope, fee and timing.

What should we include in a quote request?

Start with the pizza dimensions, reference artwork and required quantities. If you have an existing box you want to match, include its inside dimensions and a photo of the closure. Use the product quote form below to share your requirements.
