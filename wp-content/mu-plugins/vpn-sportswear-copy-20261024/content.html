Packaging for folded sportswear and activewear sets

Give jerseys, gym shirts, leggings and coordinated activewear sets a box that fits the way they are packed. Custom sportswear packaging boxes can hold a single garment or a complete set, with space for tissue, a hangtag or an insert card. The dimensions should follow your folded garments rather than a standard clothing size.

The images show a separate lid and base with a folded polo shirt inside. For a two-piece set or a thicker garment, we can adjust the internal space and presentation. If you are comparing other formats, browse our fashion and sportswear packaging collection.

Choose the box around your sales channel

A lid-and-base box suits a retail display or a gift presentation. A folding carton can be an alternative when storage space and assembly matter. Both can carry your branding, but the construction and material need to be specified separately.

For direct-to-customer deliveries, a corrugated apparel mailer is another option. A presentation box may need an outer shipping carton; we will discuss the packed weight and delivery method before recommending the shipping protection.

Size, print and finishing options

  • Fit: confirm the folded width, length and height, the number of garments, and any tissue or accessories going inside.
  • Material: paperboard, wrapped rigid board or corrugated board can be considered for different box structures. Board thickness is selected to suit the construction and packed contents.
  • Branding: add your logo, collection artwork and a clear area for size, color or barcode labels.
  • Finish: discuss matte or gloss lamination, foil stamping, embossing or spot UV according to your artwork and budget.

If your collection includes several garment sizes, send the smallest and largest folded samples. We can review whether one box size will work across the range or whether separate sizes will reduce empty space. Bulk orders, samples and quality checks

VPN manufactures custom packaging in Vietnam. Orders for this product start at 1,000 boxes. For bulk sportswear packaging, tell us the quantity for each size or design and your expected repeat orders so we can quote the production quantities you need.

We support design development and make samples to the agreed brief. Sample fees depend on the design. Sample preparation usually takes 7–15 days; production after sample approval takes 7–45 days, depending on the order. Shipping time is separate and depends on the destination and transport method.

Quality checks take place throughout production. For this type of packaging, the sample review should cover garment fit, lid fit, print and finish, and how the contents sit after packing. Finished orders can be packed in cartons or shrink-wrapped bundles, as agreed in the quotation.

Frequently asked questions

What is the minimum order quantity?

The minimum is 1,000 boxes. Please include the quantity for each design and size when requesting a quote.

Can a box hold a complete activewear set?

Yes. We can size the box for a top and leggings, a jersey set or other folded garments. The final dimensions depend on the folded contents, tissue and any inserts, and are confirmed during sampling.

Can I approve a sample before bulk production?

Yes. We prepare a sample to the agreed design so you can review the fit, structure and appearance. Fees and timing are confirmed for your project before sample work begins.

What do you need to provide a quotation?

Start with your email, order quantity, folded garment measurements and delivery destination. Add a reference photo, artwork and preferred box style if available.
