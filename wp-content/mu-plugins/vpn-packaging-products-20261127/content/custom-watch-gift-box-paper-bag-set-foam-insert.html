Set the presentation around the watch

Start with the case diameter and thickness, lug-to-lug length, crown and button positions, strap width and how the bracelet or strap fastens. Specify the intended display position and how the customer should lift the watch out. A cutout that fits the outer case still needs clearance around the crown. In a strapped presentation, check strap pressure, ease of removal and contact between the lid and the watch face.

If the set includes a card, booklet, charger or extra strap, show where each item sits in the complete pack-out. Depending on weight and presentation, the insert can include separate access cutouts or a covered compartment. Choose the foam density, facing and cavity shape after checking the actual product for movement or surface marks.

Size the paper bag for the finished rigid box

Measure the assembled box, including textured wrap and any protruding details. Use those outside dimensions to plan the bag's width, gusset and height. Allow enough loading clearance for the staff to pack the box at the counter and for the customer to lift it out. Check the handle, reinforcement and base under the weight of the filled box and any accessories.

Approve the box wrap, bag paper and logo finish as a pair. Paper texture and porosity affect how ink or foil looks, so compare physical proofs under the same lighting. For retail programs, note which brand colors, finishes and logo positions must stay consistent across sizes and reorders.

Approve the complete set before production

  • Check fit around the case, crown and strap with the lid fully closed.
  • Confirm that the watch can be lifted out without pulling against the foam or pressing a button.
  • Load the finished set into the bag and inspect handle comfort, gusset movement and stability when set down.
  • Review artwork, color, texture, finish, accessory placement and the final master-carton packing method on the sample.

Buyer questions about watch box and bag sets

Can several watch sizes share one outer box?

Yes, when dimensions and accessory needs permit. An outer box and bag can be standardized while inserts vary by watch model. Provide the range of case and strap dimensions to confirm the shared layout.

Can I coordinate the box and bag with an existing brand color?

Provide a physical reference and, where available, the specified color value. Approve both materials together because texture, paper tone and finish affect the result.
