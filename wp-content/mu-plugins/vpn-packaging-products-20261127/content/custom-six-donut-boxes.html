What fits in a six-donut tuck-top box?

This shallow paperboard carton is laid out for six donuts in two rows of three. The internal floor should suit the widest donuts and the top clearance should suit the highest icing or topping. Use an assembled sample to check fit and close the attached lid with the baked goods inside.

Fit all six donuts before choosing the box width

Lay out the six largest representative donuts as staff normally pack them. Record the outside diameter including toppings that extend beyond the doughnut. Check that the two rows can be picked up without dragging food across the rim or rubbing the next donut.

Measure the highest topping from the supporting surface, including any liner. The lid and tucked front edge need clearance during closing as well as when the box is at rest.

Check the scored folds and lid under service conditions

The lid hinges at the back and folds into the body. A physical sample lets you check the front tuck and the corner folds, including the normal small paperboard seam shown in the gallery.

Pack a mixed order as you normally would. Close the lid, check that it holds without pressing the topping, and carry or stack the box the same way as a real takeaway or delivery order.

Agree contact conditions and repeat-order details

Share whether donuts enter warm or at room temperature and how long the box stays closed. If a liner is needed, include its size and intended position so you can approve the final fit and contact surfaces together.

Send artwork quantities by bakery, outlet or seasonal design. If staff store box blanks at the counter, ask for a sample bundle and specify the packing count and outer-carton identification during quotation.

Questions before ordering

Will six large donuts or filled doughnuts fit?

Provide the largest donut diameter and height, including toppings. The six-count layout describes the number of positions. Exact capacity depends on your product dimensions and approved box size.

Can I request a food-contact liner?

Provide the food type, fill temperature, hold time and destination market. Review the liner and any required documentation for those conditions.

Is this a deep cupcake-style box?

This design uses a shallow two-row layout for donuts. A deeper carton with a shaped tray can be discussed for cupcakes and taller desserts.

How can several outlets order the same box with different artwork?

Send quantity per artwork, delivery location and any required carton labels. The quotation can list print versions and bundle counts separately.
