How does a four-slot cupcake box hold the cakes?

A raised paperboard insert supports four cupcake liners in a 2 × 2 layout. Each hole is sized to engage the liner while allowing staff to lift the cupcake out. The box height is set separately so the attached lid can close above the tallest topping.

Size the insert using your filled liners

Send a liner type or sample. Measure the base and the diameter where the liner will meet the raised tray, especially if the cup widens toward the top. Share how staff will pick up the cupcake. A hole size that works for one cup can be loose or difficult to load with another.

For four cupcakes, check that the openings keep the widest toppings apart. A tester should be able to load and remove all four pieces without catching a paper rim or brushing another decoration.

Check the lid under normal bakery handling

Run the fit check with the tallest topping in the actual insert. Close the lid and tuck the tabs as staff would during service. Check for contact with frosting, bowing that changes the fit, or a front flap that can catch on the tray.

Plan cartons and inserts for daily assembly

Tell us whether you need the box and insert bundled together or counted separately. For a flat-packed programme, record the assembly steps and ask someone from your fulfilment team to test a sample at the intended speed.

For any direct contact or oily liner, give the food type, temperature, holding time and market. Agree which surface will touch the food and which material documents are required for that use.

Questions before ordering

Will standard and large cupcakes fit the same insert?

Fit depends on how the liner widens, its diameter at insert height and the opening size. If you use two liners, provide both for a sample check.

How much height should I allow for frosting?

Measure from the underside of the liner to the highest topping. Approve clearance by closing a loaded sample using the normal tuck action.

Can I request a custom logo on the four-cupcake box?

Send exterior artwork and a print reference. Approve the placement on the closed mock-up so that the mark clears the tucked edge and corners.

Are the carton and insert packed together?

Specify the preferred bundle counts, whether staff assemble the insert and the delivery destination. Packing is agreed during the quote.
