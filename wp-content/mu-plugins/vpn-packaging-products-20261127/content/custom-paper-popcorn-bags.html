What kind of paper popcorn bag is this?

This is an open-top flexible paper bag for serving ready-popped popcorn at cinemas, concession counters and events. Its side gussets open during filling, and a folded base forms the bottom. Each size should be selected using the planned serving volume and the counter’s filling method.

Choose size by portion and counter workflow

Give your standard small, regular or large serving volume and an actual scoop size. Popcorn expands, so filled volume and mouth width tell us more about fit than weight alone. Ask staff to scoop into a test bag from a flat bundle during ordinary service.

Check whether staff can hold the mouth open, whether the bag sits on the counter as intended, and how much space is needed above the fill line to carry it. Record the expanded gusset depth on the approved drawing.

Match the paper to the topping and holding time

Plain popcorn, oil-popped popcorn and buttered popcorn can mark paper differently. Give the filling temperature, topping and expected time from serving to consumption. Use the same recipe and serving conditions on the trial bag.

Check the front, lower seams and base for visible transfer or softening after the agreed hold time. If you require direct food-contact paperwork or a specific barrier, include that requirement in the RFQ and ask which evidence is available for the selected construction.

Make the logo readable at the serving counter

Keep essential artwork away from the gusset fold and lower seam. If you sell several sizes, choose artwork or a colour cue that helps staff distinguish them in a stack.

Confirm whether your locations need mixed cartons or separate bundles by size. Ask for size and artwork codes on the outer packing to make replenishment easier.

Questions before ordering

What information helps match a paper bag to a popcorn portion?

Send the target serving volume, scoop dimensions, bag opening and desired headspace. Test the proposed size with your team during a trial fill.

Can I request grease-resistant paper for buttered popcorn?

Include the topping, fill temperature and typical hold time. The proposed paper and seam construction should be checked with your own product.

Is this bag for microwave popping?

This model is a serving bag for ready-popped popcorn. If you need microwave packaging, specify that as a separate product brief.

Can we order several sizes with one cinema logo?

Share the quantity and artwork for each size. This lets the quote show the appropriate print layout, bundle plan and minimum by version.
