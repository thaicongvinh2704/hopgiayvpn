What is a FEFCO 0201 shipping box?

A FEFCO 0201 box, also called a regular slotted carton or RSC, is a corrugated shipping box with four flaps at each end. The two long outer flaps meet along the centre seam. It is a practical starting design for warehouse packing, wholesale shipments and e-commerce orders that are sealed after loading.

Start with the packed load and inside dimensions

Measure the item with its internal retail packaging, protective pads and dividers in place. Give the gross weight of one filled shipping carton and the intended loading direction. When replacing a carton, send its inside dimensions and the most common damage or packing problem.

A tight external limit is a separate input. Include pallet dimensions, carton count per layer and any stacking or conveyor limit. Review the finished outer size after the board and closure are selected.

Choose board and printing around the shipping route

A carton that stands at a dry packing bench may face different conditions after stacking, storage and delivery. For a heavier load or a longer route, give the expected stack height, storage duration and exposure to humidity. List any compression or handling test required by your buyer.

For logo and orientation marks, identify the panels that staff must see when picking or stacking cartons. Keep scannable labels clear of the centre tape, scores and exposed corners. Approve the print position on one assembled sample.

Check fit and closure on an assembled carton

Load a sample with the real product and cushioning. Check the inner flaps, the centre seam, tape contact and any rubbing or movement inside. For repeated orders, use the approved carton and artwork as the reference for subsequent batches.

Questions before ordering

What dimensions should I send for a custom RSC quote?

Send inside length, width and height in millimetres, plus the gross weight when packed. Include a maximum outside size when working with pallets or carrier limits.

Can I add a printed company logo and handling marks?

Send your artwork and mark each panel where a logo, handling mark or label is required. The print method and board are reviewed together.

When should I compare a corrugated mailer?

A hinged mailer can suit presentation and a direct-to-customer unboxing experience. A taped RSC is a common choice for straightforward packing and distribution.

What sets the order price and minimum?

Quote details depend on size, board, ink coverage, quantity per size and artwork, packing, and delivery location. Ask for tooling and sample costs to be shown separately if applicable.
