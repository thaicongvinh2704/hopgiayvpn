Plan for the assembled beauty set

Measure the complete set as it will be handed to the customer. Include outer cartons, a rigid gift box, tissue and promotional cards rather than only the dimensions of the individual bottles. Tell us whether the same bag must hold one carton or a mixed purchase, because that changes both the footprint and the way weight is distributed.

A tall fragrance carton may need more height while a multi-item skincare set may need a wider base. Check whether the product can enter the opening without catching the handles or scraping the print. The bags shown are outer retail carriers; any inserts, leak protection or bottle cushioning belong in the agreed inner packaging.

Keep the two-tone artwork consistent at the folds

The transition from blush front to burgundy side is a production detail worth reviewing. Specify where the color break should fall relative to the side creases and seam. Share a color standard or physical brand sample for both colors and identify the logo area, border and any metallic-looking accents.

A screen image cannot confirm a paper dye, ink system or metallic process. Those choices depend on the desired finish, stock and quantity. Inspect the sample opened and flattened so you can see the side panels, top fold and bottom. This helps reveal color breaks or unprinted areas that may be less obvious in a front view.

Choose the flat handles for your beauty set

The reference shows flat, burgundy loop handles and visible attachment areas. Choose the handle material to match how the bag will be used. Specify whether you want a paper-based or textile handle, its width and length, and the corresponding attachment and reinforcement arrangement.

For sample review, use the heaviest intended boxed set and inspect the handle grip, top edge, fixing patches and base after normal carrying. Agree any required carrying test in advance, including the weight, handling conditions and acceptance criteria.

Frequently asked questions

Can the blush and burgundy colors be changed?

Yes. Send the two color references and artwork showing which panels receive each color. The final paper and print route are reviewed with a sample, particularly where the color changes at a fold.

Are the handles ribbon or paper?

The images show a flat loop-handle style. Material, width, fixing method and reinforcement must be confirmed in the quotation and sample; we do not infer fibre composition from the illustration.

Is this a bag for loose cosmetic bottles?

It is an outer retail carrier. For glass bottles or products that can leak, specify the appropriate carton, insert or other inner protection. Review the filled bag with the full set before approving it.

Can you coordinate the bag with a skincare gift box?

Yes. Share the finished box dimensions and brand references so the opening, base, print and handle direction can be reviewed together. The bag and box remain separately specified items in the quotation.
