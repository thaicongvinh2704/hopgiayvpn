Choose the footprint around your retail pack

Measure the contents as they will leave the counter: a folded shirt with tissue, a shoe box, or several smaller accessories. Specify the usable width, height and side gusset, not only the outside face. Leave enough clearance for staff to load the pack without forcing the side folds. A bag for two lightweight garments may need more space than a compact but heavier accessories order, so size and carrying requirements should be reviewed together.

The two bag proportions in the main illustration show a design direction, not a fixed size set. For a store group, list the quantity for each size and explain which purchases it serves. This makes it possible to compare a smaller range of practical sizes with a wider collection before deciding on the production brief.

Make a small logo work on black paper

Dark stock changes how a printed color looks. A metallic-looking design on screen does not establish the foil shade, coverage or rub resistance of a physical bag. Send the original logo file and identify whether you want ink, foil, embossing or a combination. Keep enough space around the mark for a clean reading distance, especially on smaller variants.

Ask to review fine lettering, foil edges and the appearance of the dark surface after folding. A coated finish and an uncoated dyed sheet feel different and can show handling marks differently. The approved physical sample should settle that choice; a monitor preview is useful for placement, but not for deciding the final surface feel.

Specify rope handles and the base as one system

The handle detail shows a rope-handle direction with a folded upper edge. Rope composition, fixing method, reinforcement and any base board are agreed for the real contents. Do not choose the construction by paper weight alone. Include the complete packed weight and normal carrying conditions so the top and base can be reviewed together.

During sample approval, load the actual retail pack and check the grip, clearance above the contents, handle fixing points and base shape. Also inspect the seam and folds after the bag has been opened and carried. Any load requirement should be documented and tested under agreed conditions rather than inferred from the product illustration.

Frequently asked questions

Can you supply these boutique bags with our own logo?

Yes. The black-and-gold reference can be adapted to your artwork, dimensions and handle direction. Send the vector logo, quantity by size and desired print or foil effect so VPN can review the specification and quote the work.

Should we choose dyed black paper or a printed black surface?

That depends on the desired feel, budget and finish. Compare the appearance of cut edges, folded corners and the logo on physical samples. We review the paper route with the artwork rather than treating every black finish as interchangeable.

Are the small and large bags in the image supplied as a set?

The images illustrate a coordinated size range. The number of sizes, quantity for each and packing arrangement are specified separately. They are not a stock assortment with fixed dimensions.

Can this bag be used to post clothing to customers?

This is a retail carrier, not a parcel mailer. If the order will travel through a courier network, specify a separate shipping pack and assess that pack with the garment and any inner box.
