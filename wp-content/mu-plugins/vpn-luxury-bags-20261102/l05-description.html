Build the dimensions around the whole gift

A corporate pack may combine a rigid box, printed card and tissue, while a lifestyle purchase may include several differently shaped items. Measure the assembled pack and identify the heaviest expected combination. Allow enough opening space for staff to load the items without bending cards or forcing the gussets.

For a program with several gift tiers, compare which items share the same bag size before ordering a large assortment. Give quantities for each size and artwork, plus any delivery batches. The two reference proportions illustrate a family of bags; they do not indicate fixed stock sizes or a prepacked retail set.

Keep olive color and fine artwork under control

Olive can appear warmer or cooler depending on the paper surface and lighting. If the bag sits beside an existing gift box, send a physical reference or agreed color target and compare the assembled packaging together. A textured sheet and a smooth printed stock can produce different results even when they share a similar screen color.

The restrained leaf-style drawing is an artwork direction, not an environmental certification. Fine line work and a small wordmark should be checked at the actual print size. Ask for the paper and printing options to be reviewed together so the mark remains legible without filling the surface with additional finishes.

Confirm handle fibre and any environmental brief

The flat handles have a woven appearance, but the illustration does not establish cotton, paper or another fibre. Tell us your preferred material and whether a particular handling or environmental requirement matters. The attachment, top reinforcement and base should suit the gift weight and the way recipients will carry it.

An olive color, leaf motif or natural-looking handle does not prove recycled content, recyclability or a certification claim. If you need those properties, state the requirement for the paper, coatings, adhesive and handles and request the relevant supporting information. Approve the complete material combination rather than relying on the visual style.

Frequently asked questions

Are the handles cotton?

The reference shows a flat woven-look handle. Exact fibre, weave, width and fixing method are selected and confirmed in the quotation and sample. Cotton should not be assumed from appearance alone.

Does the green design mean the bag is recycled or certified?

No. Color and artwork do not establish environmental properties. Specify the required material content or certification and ask for documentation for the exact agreed material combination.

Can you supply matching bags for several gift-box sizes?

Yes. Send the complete box dimensions and packed weight for each tier. We can review a coordinated bag range while confirming the quantities and specification of each size separately.

Are tissue, gift tags and inserts included?

They are included only when listed in the agreed quotation. Describe them in the brief, including size, artwork and how the gifts will be assembled, so the full packing arrangement can be reviewed.
