Specify the actual bottle, not only its volume

Two bottles marked with the same volume can have different diameters, shoulders and heights. Send the maximum body diameter or width, total height including the closure, and filled weight. If the bottle goes into a gift box first, use the closed box measurements instead. Include any tissue, neck tag or other item that needs space in the bag.

The illustration suggests a single-bottle format, but it does not establish a standard bottle size. Ask for the usable internal dimensions and check the complete pack at sample stage. If you need a two-bottle carrier, treat it as a different construction requiring a wider footprint and any necessary separation; do not simply enlarge the artwork on this narrow bag.

Review the base and rope attachments under load

Glass concentrates weight into a small footprint. Specify whether a base board, additional reinforcement or a different construction is required for your bottle. Paper grade, seam, bottom folds and handle fixing points should be reviewed together. None of these can be rated from an illustration or selected only by the apparent thickness of the paper.

Use the actual filled bottle or complete boxed gift during sample approval. Inspect the base shape, seam, top fold and handle attachments after carrying under agreed conditions. Define any required test method and acceptance criteria with the project brief. A product page does not substitute for a carrying test or establish a maximum safe load.

Separate gift presentation from transit protection

The navy face and gold-tone wordmark can coordinate with a winery label or presentation box. Share the original artwork and a physical color reference if matching existing navy packaging matters. Metallic-looking details may be printed or foiled depending on the specification, so approve the real finish rather than relying on screen color.

This is a retail gifting carrier, not a shipping container for glass. A courier or export shipment needs separately specified outer packaging and, where appropriate, bottle protection or dividers. Tell us whether you are ordering empty bags for shops or complete packed gifts so the carton packing and logistics discussion starts from the correct use.

Frequently asked questions

Will this bag fit any 750 ml wine bottle?

Not automatically. Bottle shapes vary. Send the maximum bottle dimensions and filled weight, or the dimensions of the presentation box, so the usable opening and base can be developed for that pack.

What load can the rope handles carry?

No rated load is claimed from the images. The rope, fixing points, top fold and bottom construction are selected for the contents and checked against agreed conditions using a filled sample.

Can the bag hold two bottles?

A two-bottle version needs its own footprint, handle and base review, and any suitable separation. State the bottle count and combined weight before requesting that variant.

Can this paper bag replace a protective wine shipping box?

No. It is a retail carrier. For delivery through a parcel or freight network, specify separate protective shipping packaging and assess the complete filled pack for that route.
