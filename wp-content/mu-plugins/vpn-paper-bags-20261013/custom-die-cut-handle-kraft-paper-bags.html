Questions about die cut handle paper bags

How is this handle different from a flat handle?

A die cut handle is an opening in the bag panel. A flat handle is a separate folded paper strip attached to the body. The two styles create different grip positions and usable top clearance, so compare them with the packed contents.

Can the opening shape be customized?

The opening dimensions and shape can be reviewed on the dieline. Include the user grip, surrounding paper and packed height in that review. Approve a physical sample before finalizing a custom cutout for production.

Does the reference include reinforcement?

The photos do not verify a specific reinforcement construction or material. If reinforcement is needed, define its position and composition in the quotation and approve it with the intended paper and handle opening.

What carrying weight can the bag support?

No load rating is established by the image set. Provide the real contents, weight and handling conditions so a test can be agreed for the complete bag. The cutout area and base should both be checked after that trial.

Can the logo be printed close to the handle?

It can be reviewed, but essential artwork should stay clear of the working grip and cut edge. Finalize the opening before approving the layout, and inspect the print with a hand in the opening and the bag filled.

What should I send for a quote?

Supply the item dimensions, filled weight, preferred opening, paper requirements, artwork, quantity and destination. Plan from the general 1,000-unit custom-order minimum; the exact minimum, reinforcement and sampling arrangements belong in the written offer.
