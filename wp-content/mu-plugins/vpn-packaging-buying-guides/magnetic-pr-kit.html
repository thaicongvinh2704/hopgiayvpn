Fit the magnetic PR kit box around the launch set

A rigid magnetic PR kit box suits a set that should be revealed in a specific order. Send the item list before fixing the box size. The tallest item, the location of the message card and the space below the hinged lid all affect the depth of the box and insert.

Item layout and removal
Mark the first item you want the recipient to see and the orientation of each logo. Leave space to lift items by hand, especially where the cavities sit close to the hinge.
Insert and campaign card
Compare a formed paperboard tray and EVA around the heaviest item. Include the folded card thickness and specify whether it will sit in a lid pocket or beside the products.
Branding and versions
Identify the panels that will be visible first and any foil or embossing position. If items change between campaigns, ask whether the same outer box can take a different insert.

Check the complete kit before bulk production

Load each item and place the card in its final position. Close the lid fully, check that the closure engages without pressing on the contents and lift each item out in the intended order. If kits are shipped to individual addresses, also check the packed presentation box inside its outer shipping carton.
