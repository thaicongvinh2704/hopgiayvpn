Fit the sliding drawer around the filled jars

This sleeve-and-drawer gift box presents sealed bird's nest jars in a fitted tray. The tray needs enough clearance to slide smoothly with the jars loaded, and each cavity needs enough room for the buyer to lift out a jar without pulling on the insert.

Jar and lid dimensions
Measure across the widest point, including the lid or shoulder, and record the full height with the lid on. Include filled weight and the quantity of jars per box.
Tray and pull tab
Compare a paperboard tray or EVA insert on the same filled sample. Check that the pull tab is easy to reach without rubbing a jar cap or catching under the sleeve.
Exterior and artwork
The blue-and-gold design is a reference. Specify the wrapping paper, print colors and any foil position on the sleeve and drawer front.

Approve the drawer sample with the full set

Open and close the loaded drawer several times. Check for tray flex, rubbing at the sleeve opening and any contact between jars as the tray moves. Confirm that the drawer front closes flush and that each jar can be lifted out comfortably. Use the approved tray layout and finish as the reference for repeat orders.
