Size a perfume folding carton for one filled bottle

This printed retail carton is built around an individual fragrance bottle. Send a filled sample or a dimensioned drawing that includes the cap, widest body and base profile. For a collection with several scents, identify which bottles can use the same dieline and which need a separate carton.

Carton bottom and packing sequence
Compare the proposed closure with the actual filled weight and the direction the bottle will be loaded. Approve the bottom with a filled sample before confirming the dieline for production.
Bottle support and cap clearance
Check whether a folded paperboard insert helps hold the bottle in position. Ensure the flap closes without pressing on the cap and the bottle does not catch at the opening when removed.
Artwork and SKU changes
Mark the front, barcode and variant panels on the agreed dieline. Send quantities by scent or size, especially when cartons share a structure but use different artwork.

Check the assembled carton before placing a bulk order

Fold the sample, load the bottle and close the top and bottom. Inspect the base and side seam, open the carton again and confirm the bottle comes out without scuffing its cap or label. Use the approved physical sample to confirm paperboard, print and closure before the production run.
