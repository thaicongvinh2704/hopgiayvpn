Size the corrugated gift mailer for the packed set

Start with the largest gift combination and the total packed weight. Confirm the difference between the usable space inside the mailer and the outside dimensions that will be used for packing and delivery. The layout should have enough room for the gifts and any wrap while limiting movement once the box is closed.

Separate items that can touch in transit
Map bottles, boxed items and accessories separately. Compare a paperboard divider or a shaped corrugated insert where heavier items could slide into lighter ones.
Choose the board with the filled sample
Discuss kraft E-flute or white corrugated board around the set weight and delivery plan. Check the depth available after the divider and wrap have been added.
Reserve space for shipping labels
Place the gift message and campaign print on the agreed panels. If the mailer will travel without an outer carton, identify where the shipping label and seal will go before approving the artwork.

Approve a packed mailer in its shipping configuration

Assemble, load and close the sample using the same steps the packing team will follow. Check for shifting contents, closure gaps and board deformation after handling in the expected orientations. Confirm with the fulfilment team whether the finished mailer needs another outer carton or a different packing arrangement.
