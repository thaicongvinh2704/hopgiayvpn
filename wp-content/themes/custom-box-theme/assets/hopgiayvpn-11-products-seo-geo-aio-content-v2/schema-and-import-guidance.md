# SEO, GEO and AIO implementation guidance

1. Keep normal technical SEO: crawlable links, canonical URLs, sitemap inclusion, descriptive image filenames/alt text, fast mobile rendering, and one indexable product URL per intent.
2. Use a single accurate `Product` structured-data object generated by WooCommerce/Rank Math or the theme. Do not publish a second duplicate object. Populate only supported facts. Omit fabricated `Offer`, price, availability, aggregate rating, review, GTIN, certification, or shipping data.
3. AI visibility does not require a special “AI schema,” `llms.txt`, artificial chunking, or keyword stuffing. The direct answers, comparison tables, descriptive headings, product DNA, buyer limits, and FAQs in these files make each entity easier for people and machines to interpret.
4. Keep each visible product name, slug, SEO title, description, image alt text, specs, breadcrumbs, and schema name consistent.
5. Product 08 stays Draft until the incomplete two-image gallery and factual production details are expanded and reviewed.
6. Food and ampoule pages deliberately use conditional compliance language. Add a certification or compliance statement only when it applies to the exact material/order and supporting records are available.
7. After import, validate the rendered visible word count, source HTML, internal links, image URLs, mobile layout, canonical, robots directive, XML sitemap, and structured data.
