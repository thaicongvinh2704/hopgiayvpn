A custom printed churros takeaway box keeps a serving together, gives the customer a branded handoff, and can be configured for counter service or short-distance delivery. The pictured tuck-top corrugated format includes locking tabs, an open food view, and a flat dieline, making structure and assembly easy to evaluate. Size should be based on churros length, serving count, topping or dip arrangement, grease level, hold time, and whether steam must escape. VPN can customize dimensions, food-side material, ventilation, closures, exterior printing, and assembly format. Food-contact suitability is not automatic: the final board, coating, ink placement, adhesives, and declarations must match the destination market and whether food touches the package directly.
