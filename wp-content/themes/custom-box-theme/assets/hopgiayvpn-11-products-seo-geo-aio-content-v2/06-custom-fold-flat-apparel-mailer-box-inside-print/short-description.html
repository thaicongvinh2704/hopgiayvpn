A custom fold-flat apparel mailer box with inside print turns the first open view into a branded fashion moment while keeping warehouse storage efficient. The one-piece corrugated structure arrives flat, locks into shape during packing, and can hold folded shirts, knitwear, activewear, scarves, or coordinated clothing sets. Box dimensions should follow the finished fold, tissue wrap, size range, number of garments, and compression tolerance rather than a generic clothing-box size. VPN can customize the exterior, inside-lid artwork, dimensions, flute, closure, tissue, stickers, and paper-based inserts. Before production, confirm that dark garments do not rub against sensitive print or coatings, that the loaded lid closes without crushing the fold, and that the package performs through the planned parcel route. Send folded-product dimensions, weight, SKU range, pack-out photos, artwork, quantity, destination, and shipping method for quotation.
