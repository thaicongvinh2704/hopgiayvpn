# Research basis

The content strategy follows current primary guidance and avoids unsupported manufacturing claims.

- Google Search Central — AI features and your website: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features
- Google Search Central — AI optimization guide: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide
- Google Search Central — helpful, reliable, people-first content: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content
- Google Search Central — Product structured data: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/product
- Google Search Central — image SEO: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/google-images
- ISTA — packaged-product test procedures: https://ista.org/test_procedures.php
- U.S. FDA — food packaging and food-contact substances: https://www.fda.gov/food/food-ingredients-packaging/food-packaging-other-substances-come-contact-food-information-consumers
- European Commission — food-contact materials: https://food.ec.europa.eu/food-safety/chemical-safety/food-contact-materials_en

Practical rule used throughout: specify, sample, and test the exact pack for its declared market and route. The articles do not claim ISTA testing, food-contact compliance, pharmaceutical approval, certification, fixed lead time, fixed price, or performance unless order-specific evidence can support it.
