Pizza-box questions to resolve before ordering

Why must this page remain Draft?

Only two images are available and the gallery does not yet document closure, interior, vents, edges, or print details. Keeping it in Draft prevents an incomplete product presentation from being published.

Are pizza boxes automatically food safe?

No. Food-use suitability depends on the complete material and converting specification, actual contact, temperature, grease, and destination regulations—not the box name.

How are vents selected?

Test vent area and position with the recipe, temperature, hold time, stack, climate, and delivery bag. The goal is a controlled trade-off between condensation and heat retention.

What controls stacking performance?

Board and flute, span, humidity, vent cutouts, loaded weight, stack height, handling, and time all matter. Evaluate boxes with hot, packed pizzas under realistic conditions.
