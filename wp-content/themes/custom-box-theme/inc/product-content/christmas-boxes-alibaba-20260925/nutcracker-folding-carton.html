Product fit and any required interior support

The carton is shown empty with a plain inside. Specify the contents' dimensions, weight, shape, and orientation before choosing a fitment. A lightweight decorative item may need only a folded paper support, while a fragile ornament could require a cradle and a separate shipper. Confectionery may need a sealed inner package. A tall carton must prevent the item from sliding vertically without using so much filler that the closing flaps press on the product.

For a set with a card or multiple pieces, sketch each position and the order of packing. Confirm finger access and whether staff can assemble the carton quickly at the expected volume. Check clearance at the top and bottom, surface contact points, movement during carrying, and how the package looks on a shelf.
