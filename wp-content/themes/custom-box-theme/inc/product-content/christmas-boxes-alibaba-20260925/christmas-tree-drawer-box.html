Sampling and quality-control checkpoints

Start with a plain sleeve and tray to verify dimensions, clearance, squareness, tray depth, extension, pull-tab position, and load. Then apply the artwork and check stripe alignment around corners, tree centering, garland color, registration, scuffing, seam visibility, and red consistency. Open and close the filled sample repeatedly, checking that the tray stays aligned and that the contents do not shift when the box is carried. Evaluate the product removal sequence from the recipient's perspective as well as the packing team's.

Write measurable acceptance criteria for tray movement, dimensional variation, sleeve fit, tab bond, printed position, finish defects, and insert placement. Retain the signed artwork and physical approval sample with a revision identifier. During production, inspect representative units and record any deviation from the agreed reference. Complete a packed carton trial if units will be stacked or exported. Check carton count, cushioning, shipping marks, pallet arrangement, and whether the drawer tab could catch or tear during transit.
