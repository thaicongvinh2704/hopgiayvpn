Set a safe handle load before artwork approval

Think through retail carry and shipping together

The handle makes the format convenient to hand over, but it also creates a point that can catch during carton packing or shelf storage. Decide whether the cartons ship assembled or flat, whether they are nested, and how the handle slot is protected from dust or pressure. A filled carry box may still need a master carton or outer shipper for parcel delivery. The outer packaging should carry the distribution load so the decorative handle is not mistaken for a shipping fixture.

Confirm finished dimensions, packed count, unit orientation, carton markings and gross weight using actual samples. Test the top after compression in the planned master carton; repeated pressure can flatten a gable or distort the handle. Check whether a retailer needs a hanging feature or shelf display, but do not add a hang tab without verifying how that changes the folding pattern and load path. Shelf labels and barcodes should not land on a curved or folded panel.
