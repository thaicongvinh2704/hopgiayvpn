# Custom Greaseproof Bakery Paper Bags

Keep butter and oil from distracting from the presentation. Custom greaseproof bakery paper bags are developed to reduce visible grease transfer when packing croissants, donuts, cookies, pastries and other rich baked goods.

VPN Packaging Factory produces custom sizes and printed grease-resistant paper bags in Vietnam for bakery chains, cafés, food brands, importers and packaging distributors. The paper and bag structure should be selected according to the food, contact time and packing conditions—not by appearance alone.

## Greaseproof Is a Performance Requirement

Standard paper can absorb fats and develop translucent marks. Grease-resistant paper is designed to slow that transfer, helping the bag remain cleaner during handling and customer takeaway. Required performance varies widely: a dry roll, a butter croissant and a fried pastry do not need the same specification.

Suitable applications include:

- Croissants and laminated pastries
- Donuts and fried bakery products
- Cookies and butter-rich biscuits
- Danish pastries and filled buns
- Savory pastries, pies and turnovers
- Sandwiches and bakery snacks with oily fillings

Provide the product’s oil level, surface temperature and expected contact time so an appropriate material can be proposed.

## Custom Bag Formats

Greaseproof paper can be evaluated for several bag structures:

- Flat bags for single pastries and quick service
- Side gusset bags for thicker baked goods
- Square bottom bags for multi-item orders
- Open-top bags for counter packing
- Fold-over or sticker-close bags
- Window bags, subject to material compatibility

Custom width, gusset and length should leave enough room for easy insertion without excessive empty space. If sharp crusts or heavy products are involved, puncture and seam strength must also be considered.

## Paper Selection and Barrier Testing

“Greaseproof” should not be treated as a universal promise. Paper grade, converting, folds, glue lines and printed areas can all influence finished-bag performance. Test the complete bag, not only an unconverted paper sample.

During approval, evaluate:

- Visible grease staining over the intended holding period
- Transfer at folds, seams and pressure points
- Paper strength when exposed to warm or moist food
- Odor and appearance under intended use
- Suitability for direct food contact where required
- Required documentation for the destination market

If your product is unusually oily, an inner wrap or alternative barrier construction may be more appropriate. The specification should reflect the use case rather than an unsupported environmental or performance claim.

## Custom Printing on Grease-Resistant Paper

Print your logo, bakery pattern or short customer message on the main panels. Natural and white appearances may be available depending on the selected paper. Because paper surface and barrier treatment can affect ink behavior, color, drying and rub resistance should be checked on the production material.

VPN Packaging Factory can review print-ready artwork, provide a dieline and recommend a sampling route. Keep critical information clear of seams and folds, and confirm any food-contact limitations for printed areas.

## A Cleaner Handover for Branded Bakery Orders

Grease control helps the bag look presentable when it reaches the customer, but operational fit is equally important. Staff should be able to open and fill the bag quickly without touching the food excessively. A correctly sized bag can reduce folding, squeezing and unnecessary paper use.

For chains and distributors, define an approved specification by SKU: finished dimensions, paper code, structure, print target, carton quantity and labeling. Finished bags can be supplied flat with export-ready packing.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the difference between greaseproof and standard kraft paper?

Greaseproof paper is selected to resist fat penetration more effectively than standard untreated paper. Actual performance depends on the grade and finished construction.

### Are greaseproof bags suitable for hot pastries?

Potential suitability depends on temperature, moisture, fat content and contact time. State the packing conditions and test the bag with the actual product.

### Can greaseproof paper bags be custom printed?

Yes. Artwork, ink coverage and color should be evaluated on the selected paper, including rub resistance and any direct-food-contact requirements.

### Can I add a clear window?

A window may be possible, but the window material, adhesive and surrounding paper must be assessed as one construction.

### How do I compare two greaseproof samples?

Use the same food, temperature, contact time and pressure. Inspect the paper, folds and seams for staining or loss of strength.
