Start with the packed product and usable interior

A mailer should be sized around everything that goes inside it, not only the main item. Include the retail package, protective wrap, insert, leaflet and any promotional card when measuring. Allow enough clearance for packing and removal without giving the contents unnecessary room to move. A shallow accessory set may need a different footprint from several small bottles even when the total volume is similar.

Specify internal length, width and height clearly. External measurements are useful for warehouse and shipping planning, but they cannot replace usable internal dimensions. Board thickness, folded walls and the closure all affect the available space. If you only have the product measurements, send them with a photo and an explanation of how you would like the items arranged. We can then review a practical starting layout for the sample.
