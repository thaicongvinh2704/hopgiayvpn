Build a birthday assortment that works on the shelf

A birthday bag range needs more planning than one attractive front panel. A party shop may need small bags for boxed jewelry and larger bags for toys; a gift distributor may need coordinated designs across several price points. Define those groups before selecting dimensions. Measure the actual gift or its retail box, then allow room for loading, tissue and the handle attachment area. A bag that is too narrow can distort an otherwise carefully printed design.

For a mixed assortment, list each size, artwork version and quantity separately. This makes the quotation, sampling and carton labels easier to compare. If you want an assorted retail pack, specify the number of bags from each design in that pack. The two examples shown are a starting point for that discussion, not a promise that every order automatically includes both sizes or every printed pattern.

Think about how the collection will be presented before adding finishing details. Bags sold individually may need a retail identifier on each piece, while a wholesale carton may need the design and size listed on its outer label. If your stores display bags on a fixture, provide that fixture's requirements for review. A handle shape that works well for carrying does not automatically establish compatibility with every display. Include any required gift tag, tissue color or retail sleeve in the brief as a separate item so the approved assortment is unambiguous.

Paper handles, top folds and a base suited to the gift

The reference birthday bags use white paper loop handles, a folded top edge and expandable side panels. These features should be considered together. The top fold provides a location for the handle attachment, while the side panels provide space for a boxed gift. The base must open consistently so staff can load the bag without forcing its seams. The visible handle shape is a design reference, not a tested carrying capacity.

Tell us the weight and shape of the heaviest intended gift. For a heavier boxed item, we can review paper selection, handle attachment and reinforcement with the sample. For a lighter party favor, an unnecessarily heavy specification may increase cost without improving the customer experience. A filled sample is more useful than an empty bag when checking handle comfort, seam behavior and how the bag stands during packing.

Keep birthday artwork readable across several sizes

The colorful reference layouts work as a family because their stripes, greeting and decorative elements remain recognizable. Your artwork can use a different greeting, palette, illustration or brand identity. Decide which elements must stay consistent across the collection and which may change. A short greeting often needs a different scale on a narrow bag than on a broad one; shrinking the entire layout can make small lettering difficult to read.

Provide vector artwork where available, along with any color references and approved translations. We can support the design process and prepare a layout for review. Keep important lettering away from folds, seams and handle attachment zones. If a barcode, retail label or importer information is required, reserve a suitable panel before the illustration is finalized. Confirm that you have permission to reproduce any supplied characters, logos or artwork.

Choose paper and finishing around the retail brief

A white printing surface is a useful starting point for a colorful birthday collection. Paper grade, thickness and finishing are selected for the artwork, bag size, contents and budget. A natural matte appearance and a more polished printed finish create different shelf impressions. If you need a specific tactile finish, send a physical reference or describe the result rather than relying only on a screen image.

Special effects such as selected foil details or an added coating can be discussed during quotation. They should be specified by area and purpose because they affect production steps and cost. Ask for the material and finish to be identified in the quotation. Recycled content, certification or environmental claims require supporting documentation for the chosen specification; the pictured paper appearance alone does not establish any of those claims.

Plan bulk supply by design, size and destination

For birthday gift bags wholesale, the total order quantity is only the beginning. The distribution of that quantity matters: one large run of a single design is different from several smaller runs across many sizes. State the expected first order and any likely repeat demand so we can review an appropriate production plan. The general planning minimum is 1,000 units; confirm the minimum for each selected size and artwork in your quotation.

Birthday packaging can be sold throughout the year, but a retailer's range launch may have a fixed arrival date. Work backward from that date and separate sampling, production and transport. Sample preparation takes approximately 7–15 days depending on the order. Production takes approximately 7–45 days after sample approval, depending on the order. Transit and any import clearance are additional considerations, so share the delivery country, destination and required arrival date early.
