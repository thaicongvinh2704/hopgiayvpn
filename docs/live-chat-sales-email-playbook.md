# 30 tình huống live chat — mời khách để lại email

Cập nhật: 06/10/2026. Dành cho VPN Packaging / Tho Nguyen, Sale Manager.

## Mục tiêu và cách dùng

Mục tiêu chuyển đổi duy nhất: khách tự nguyện cung cấp email để sales tiếp tục tư vấn dự án. Bộ câu hỏi được đề xuất từ FAQ và phạm vi sản phẩm công khai, chưa phải thống kê tần suất từ lịch sử chat thực tế. Câu trả lời tiếng Anh phù hợp website hiện tại; tiêu đề tiếng Việt giúp nhân viên chọn nhanh.

Trả lời đúng câu hỏi trước, sau đó mời nhận một kết quả cụ thể qua email. Mỗi lượt chỉ nên hỏi email và tối đa một thông tin dự án. Nếu khách đã cung cấp đủ dữ liệu thì bỏ câu hỏi bổ sung. Chấp nhận email cá nhân, không bắt buộc email công ty.

Nếu email đã có trong hội thoại, không hỏi lại: thay lời mời bằng “Thanks — we have your email. Our sales team will follow up with the details.” Chỉ nói sẽ gửi tài liệu/báo giá khi đội sales có thể thực hiện; không nói đã gửi khi chưa gửi. Các mẫu dưới đây là nội dung soạn sẵn, chưa được cài thành bot hoặc tự động gửi email.

## A. Giá, số lượng và mẫu thử

### 01. Giá một hộp / xin báo giá

**Khách:** “How much do your custom boxes cost?”

**Trả lời:** “Pricing depends on the box size, style and quantity. Approximately how many boxes do you need? Leave your email in the field above and our sales team can follow up with a quote for your project.”

### 02. Số lượng đặt tối thiểu

**Khách:** “What is your MOQ?”

**Trả lời:** “The minimum order depends on the box style and customization. Which type of box are you considering? What’s the best email for us to send the minimum quantity and pricing options?”

### 03. Đơn nhỏ / thương hiệu mới

**Khách:** “I’m starting a small brand. Can I order 100 boxes?”

**Trả lời:** “We can check whether that quantity works for your packaging style and suggest alternatives if needed. Leave your email above so our team can follow up with a suitable small-order option.”

### 04. Muốn xem mẫu trước khi đặt hàng

**Khách:** “Can I get a sample before placing an order?”

**Trả lời:** “Yes, sampling can be arranged before bulk production. What product will the box hold? Share your email and we can follow up with the sample options and next steps.”

### 05. Mẫu miễn phí / phí mẫu

**Khách:** “Are samples free?”

**Trả lời:** “Sample availability, customization fees and shipping costs need to be confirmed for your request. Leave your email above and our team can explain the available sample options and any costs before you decide.”

## B. Thời gian và giao hàng

### 06. Thời gian sản xuất

**Khách:** “How long does production take?”

**Trả lời:** “Production time depends on the design, quantity and finishing, after approval of the specifications. When do you need the packaging? Share your email so our team can follow up with a project-specific timeline.”

### 07. Đơn gấp / ngày ra mắt

**Khách:** “I need the boxes urgently. Can you deliver before my launch?”

**Trả lời:** “We’ll need to check production and shipping against your deadline before confirming. What date do you need them by? Leave your email above so our team can follow up with the feasible options.”

### 08. Có giao đến quốc gia của khách không

**Khách:** “Do you ship to the USA / UK / my country?”

**Trả lời:** “We work with international B2B buyers and can review delivery options for your destination. Which country are you in? Share your email so our team can confirm the shipping options for your order.”

### 09. Phí vận chuyển / tổng chi phí

**Khách:** “How much is shipping? Is it included in the price?”

**Trả lời:** “Shipping depends on the packed volume, destination and delivery method. What’s your delivery city or postcode? Leave your email so we can follow up with a quote showing the shipping scope and what is included.”

### 10. Có thể gửi catalogue không

**Khách:** “Can you send me your catalogue?”

**Trả lời:** “You can browse the products on our website now. Which packaging category interests you? Share your email and our sales team can follow up with relevant product references for your project.”

## C. Thiết kế và cấu tạo

### 11. In logo riêng

**Khách:** “Can you print my logo on the boxes?”

**Trả lời:** “Yes, custom logo printing is available. Which box style are you looking for? Leave your email above so we can follow up with the artwork requirements and suitable printing options.”

### 12. Chưa có thiết kế

**Khách:** “I don’t have a design. Can you help?”

**Trả lời:** “We can help review the box structure and artwork direction. What product are you packaging? Share your email so our team can explain the design support and next steps for your project.”

### 13. Làm kích thước riêng

**Khách:** “Can you make a box in my exact size?”

**Trả lời:** “Yes, custom dimensions can be reviewed for your product. What are the product measurements? Leave your email above so our team can follow up with a suitable box size and specification.”

### 14. Chọn giấy / độ dày

**Khách:** “Which paper material or thickness should I choose?”

**Trả lời:** “The right material depends on your product’s weight, protection needs and presentation. What will go inside the box? Share your email so our team can follow up with suitable material options.”

### 15. Ép kim, dập nổi, UV

**Khách:** “Can you do gold foil, embossing or spot UV?”

**Trả lời:** “Yes, those finishes can be reviewed for suitable materials and artwork. Which effect do you prefer? Leave your email so our team can follow up with finish references and pricing for your design.”

### 16. Màu thương hiệu / Pantone

**Khách:** “Can you match my brand color?”

**Trả lời:** “We can review your color target and printing method, with proof or sample approval before production. Do you have a Pantone reference? Share your email so our team can explain the color approval process.”

### 17. Khay lót / bảo vệ sản phẩm

**Khách:** “Can you add an insert to hold my product?”

**Trả lời:** “Yes, custom inserts can be reviewed around your product’s shape and protection needs. What product will the insert hold? Leave your email so our team can follow up with a suitable insert direction.”

## D. Loại sản phẩm và ngành hàng

### 18. Hộp nam châm / hộp quà cao cấp

**Khách:** “Do you make magnetic gift boxes?”

**Trả lời:** “Yes, magnetic rigid boxes are part of our packaging range. What will the gift set contain? Share your email so our team can follow up with relevant box styles and customization options.”

### 19. Mỹ phẩm / nước hoa / nến

**Khách:** “Can you make packaging for skincare, perfume or candles?”

**Trả lời:** “We can review custom paper packaging for your product and brand presentation. What are you packaging? Leave your email above so our team can follow up with suitable structures and finishing ideas.”

### 20. Trang sức / đồng hồ

**Khách:** “Do you make jewelry or watch gift boxes?”

**Trả lời:** “We can review gift box styles and inserts for jewelry or watches. Which product do you need to present? Share your email so our team can follow up with relevant packaging references and options.”

### 21. Thực phẩm / bánh / pizza

**Khách:** “Can I use your boxes for food or bakery products?”

**Trả lời:** “We can review food packaging requests. Please tell us the food type and whether it touches the packaging directly. Leave your email so our team can confirm the material requirements and available documentation for your project.”

### 22. Túi giấy / bộ hộp và túi đồng bộ

**Khách:** “Can you make matching paper bags for my boxes?”

**Trả lời:** “Yes, custom paper bags can be coordinated with your box design. What size or product weight should the bag accommodate? Share your email so our team can follow up with matching paper, handle and print options.”

### 23. Bao bì thân thiện môi trường

**Khách:** “Do you offer eco-friendly or recyclable packaging?”

**Trả lời:** “We can review kraft, recycled paper and other material directions for your packaging. What sustainability requirement matters most to you? Leave your email so our team can follow up with suitable options and their specifications.”

### 24. Chứng nhận / FSC

**Khách:** “Can you provide FSC-certified packaging or certificates?”

**Trả lời:** “Certificate availability and scope need to be checked for the specific material and order. Which certificate does your buyer require? Share your email so our team can confirm whether the required documentation is available.”

## E. Chuẩn bị đơn hàng và xử lý băn khoăn

### 25. Gửi file / loại file thiết kế

**Khách:** “What artwork format do you need? Can I upload my design here?”

**Trả lời:** “This chat accepts text only. We can explain how to provide your artwork and check the file requirements with you. Leave your email above so our team can send the artwork instructions.”

### 26. Giá cao / muốn giảm chi phí

**Khách:** “Your price is too high. Can you make it cheaper?”

**Trả lời:** “We can review simpler structures, materials or finishes to help fit your budget. What price range are you targeting? Share your email so our team can follow up with alternative specifications to compare.”

### 27. Bán sỉ / đại lý / đặt nhiều

**Khách:** “Do you offer wholesale pricing? I’m a distributor.”

**Trả lời:** “We work with B2B buyers and can quote against your specifications and quantities. What quantity are you considering? Leave your email so our team can follow up with a wholesale quotation for review.”

### 28. Đặt hàng / thanh toán

**Khách:** “How do I place an order, and what are your payment terms?”

**Trả lời:** “We first review the specifications and quotation, then confirm approvals and order terms before production. Share your email so our sales team can follow up with the ordering steps and payment terms for your project.”

### 29. Đặt lại / sao chép mẫu hộp cũ

**Khách:** “Can you reproduce my existing box or help with a repeat order?”

**Trả lời:** “We can review your existing specifications or previous order reference before confirming a match. Share your email so our team can explain the information needed and follow up with a reorder quote.”

### 30. Chào hỏi / không có người trả lời ngay

**Khách:** “Hi, is anyone there? I need help with packaging.”

**Trả lời:** “Thanks for reaching out! What packaging are you looking for? Please leave your email in the field above so our sales team can follow up with the details as soon as possible.”

## Khi khách không muốn cung cấp email

Không chặn chat hoặc liên tục hỏi lại. Dùng: “No problem — you can keep chatting here. What packaging do you need?” Sau khi có thêm yêu cầu cụ thể, chỉ mời lại nếu có giá trị mới, ví dụ báo giá theo kích thước hoặc phương án mẫu.

Không tự cam kết giá, MOQ, mẫu miễn phí, ngày giao chắc chắn, chứng nhận, điều kiện thanh toán hay thời hạn phản hồi chưa được sales xác nhận. Không nói “I’m Tho and I’m checking now” nếu đây là bot. Nếu dùng tự động, hiển thị nhãn “Automatic reply”.
